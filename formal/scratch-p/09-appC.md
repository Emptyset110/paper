## Appendix C. The Factorization Case Analysis

This appendix carries the per-rule content of Theorem 3 (CF3): the full case analysis over the ten rules of Table 1 (C.1–C.10) and the coeffect operations inside iterator landings (C.11). The assembly of the seven CF obligations is Theorem 3's proof in §6.1.

**C.0 Scheme and reuse discipline.** For each rule we give: **(W)** the window read off the row and its premises; **(K)** the occurrence decomposition $\kappa_r$, against the alphabet of Appendix D; **(G)** the proof that each premise is a Boolean function of the occurrence support; **(U)** the proof that the row's write is $\mathrm{lift}_r$ of per-occurrence updates, with $\widehat U_r$ enumerated; **(E)** emissions; **(Q)** equivariance and frame, by Lemmas 2–3; **(C)** the no-cardinality check for the row: an explicit inventory of every symbol the row and its premises contain, verifying that none is an arithmetic or cardinality read. The check (C) is done separately for all ten rows.

Genuinely symmetric sub-arguments are written once in full at their first occurrence and afterwards cited with their delta: the *per-key view-relation decomposition* first appears at L-Iter (C.5) and is reused at L-Finish, L-Divert, L-Leave; the *existential-witness fold* first appears at O-Insert (C.1) and is reused at O-Remove (children) and L-Unload (reliance); the *per-key satisfaction product* appears at L-Begin (C.4).

*Integration note.* The (K) items below originally cited the proof program's internal draft alphabets; per the program's own resolution rule (discrepancies between the proof deliverable's occurrence descriptions and the signature deliverable's tables resolve to the tables, obligation (D-H)), they are stated here against Appendix D's finalized alphabets, whose granularity refines the drafts in ten logged places (e.g. the three-way parent class at O-Insert, the refusing letter at O-Retire, the step-class partition among L-Iter/L-Finish/L-Raise). The mathematical content of each item is unchanged.

### C.1 O-Insert

Row (Table 1): $\theta_n$ undefined $\to \mathsf{Inactive}(\bot)$, $\Psi^t = \mathrm{id}_\Gamma$, control fields edited: $\mathrm{dom}(F_\gamma)$. Premises: $n \notin \mathrm{dom}(F_\gamma)$; $\pi \in \mathrm{dom}(F_\gamma) \cup \{\mathsf{root}\}$; $(d,p,e) \in \mathfrak C_\Gamma$; $\forall m \in \mathrm{dom}(F_\gamma).\ p \cap p_m = \varnothing$.

**(W)** Window: the requested name $n$ and, for freshness, the name set $\mathrm{dom}(F_\gamma)$ read *only through per-fiber name equalities* $[m = n]$; the requested parent $\pi$ and the per-fiber equalities $[m = \pi]$ plus the constant test $[\pi = \mathsf{root}]$; the component data $(d,p,e)$; and, for disjointness, for each present $m$ and each $k$, the memberships $[k \in p]$, $[k \in p_m]$. Frame: the (finite) index set of present fibers for the two folds; no order data.

**(K)** Per Appendix D.1's alphabet, $\kappa_{\text{O-Insert}}(W)$ contains: one scalar occurrence recording the atoms $\langle \mathsf{fresh?}, \mathsf{parent\mbox{-}class}, \mathsf{comp\mbox{-}wf?} \rangle$ (the last: $(d,p,e) \in \mathfrak C_\Gamma$, a property of the submitted data alone) together with the equality pattern among $\{n, \pi, \mathsf{root}\}$ and the requested names; and one *conflict-witness occurrence* $\mathsf{conflict}(k\text{-pattern})$ for each pair $(m,k)$ with $k \in p \cap p_m$ — a singleton-key witness recording the key's equality pattern against $d \cup p$, not the identity of $m$ or the number of such pairs. Freshness is recorded as an atom but its false polarity is marked *interface-unrealizable*: names are system-drawn (Def. 44's discipline; Def. 47 draws a name not already in use), so **(D-D)** Appendix D.1 lists the $\mathsf{fresh?}{=}0$ vectors as unreachable at the interface, and no canonical experiment attempts them.

**(G)** Premise 1 is the negation of the existential $\exists m \in \mathrm{dom}(F_\gamma).\ m = n$; each disjunct is one per-fiber equality; the premise holds iff the scalar atom $\mathsf{fresh?}$ — defined as the value of that fold — is $1$. It is a function of the occurrence support: the fold's value is recorded in the scalar occurrence, and no multiplicity enters (two fibers cannot share the name $n$, and even the *fold itself* only asks existence). Premise 2 is the disjunction $[\pi = \mathsf{root}] \vee \exists m.\ m = \pi$, likewise recorded in the parent-class atom. Premise 3 reads the submitted data alone. Premise 4 — the representative *existential-witness fold*, written out once in full: the premise is $\neg\exists (m,k).\ m \in \mathrm{dom}(F_\gamma) \wedge k \in p \wedge k \in p_m$. By construction of $\kappa$, the witness set of this existential is in bijection with the conflict-witness occurrences of $\kappa(W)$: an occurrence is placed *iff* a witness exists at its class, so

$$\text{premise 4 holds} \iff \mathrm{supp}(\kappa(W)) \text{ contains no occurrence of class } \mathsf{conflict}(\cdot).$$

This is a function of the support alone; in particular it does not depend on *how many* conflicting pairs exist — the guard is an absence test, insensitive to multiplicity. (This bijection between witnesses and witness occurrences is the single fact every reuse of this fold cites.)

**(U)** $\widehat U_{\text{O-Insert}} = \{\mathsf{new\mbox{-}entry}\}$: the write is the single registry extension $F[n \mapsto \langle d,p,e,\pi,\varnothing,\bot,\mathsf{Inactive}(\bot)\rangle]$ — one entry, whose every field is either submitted data ($d,p,e,\pi$) or a reserved constant ($\varnothing, \bot, \mathsf{Inactive}(\bot)$). $\mathrm{lift}$ is the one-point registry union; there is no per-key or per-item content. $\Psi^t = \mathrm{id}_\Gamma$ per the row, so no state map runs.

**(E)** No ordinary event; on a false premise, the refusal event and refused-input log entry carrying the failing premise's reason $\varrho$ (Definition 8(1)).

**(Q)** Equivariance: Lemma 2 (its name part is Lemma 56's own case for O-Insert: freshness and the $\forall m.\ p \cap p_m$ premise compare names/keys by equality only). Frame: Lemma 3 — a fiber added outside the window must in particular claim no key of $p$ (else it *is* a conflict witness and lies inside the window), and then premises 1–4 and the one-point write are untouched.

**(C)** No-cardinality check for the row. The row's premises contain the symbols: $\notin$, $\in$, $\cup$, $\{\mathsf{root}\}$, $\mathfrak C_\Gamma$, $\forall$, $\cap$, $=\varnothing$. The conclusion contains: $[\,\cdot \mapsto \cdot\,]$ (registry extension) and the constant tuple. There is no $|{\cdot}|$, no order relation, no arithmetic operation, and no bound mentioning a number. The only "for all" quantifies over present fibers and is discharged as an absence-of-witness test. **Verified: this row is cardinality-free.**

### C.2 O-Retire

Row: $\theta_n$ unconstrained $\to$ unchanged, $\Psi^t = \mathrm{id}_\Gamma$, edits $\tau_n$. Premise: $n \in \mathrm{dom}(F_\gamma)$.

**(W)** Window: presence of $n$ (per-fiber name equalities $[m = n]$) and the field $\tau_n$ it writes. Frame: none.

**(K)** Per Appendix D.2's alphabet: one scalar occurrence $\langle \mathsf{present?} \rangle$ plus the recorded state class $\mathrm{cls}(\theta_n) \in \{\mathsf{pending}, \mathsf{installed}, \mathsf{failed}\}$ (that is: $\mathsf{Inactive}(\bot)$ / $\theta_n \neq \mathsf{Inactive}(-)$ / $\mathsf{Inactive}(\xi)$). The class is *not read by the guard* — O-Retire is unconditional on the fiber's state ([SZC26] p. 31: "O-Retire is unconditional on the fiber's state because retiring is a request") — it is carried so that the canonical experiments for the three classes exist separately and the verdict can pin the (identical) decision at each; this is bookkeeping in $\Sigma_r$, not a guard read, and CF3 permits reads of recorded atoms (they are inside $\Sigma_r$).

**(G)** The single premise is the existential presence fold, a function of the scalar atom $\mathsf{present?}$, exactly as in C.1's premise 1 with polarity reversed. No other premise exists; in particular the guard is constant in $\mathrm{cls}(\theta_n)$ — which is itself the checkable content of the row ("unconditional") that the three per-class experiments witness.

**(U)** $\widehat U = \{\tau \mapsto \top\}$: one field assignment at one fiber. $\mathrm{lift}$ is trivial. **(E)** none / refusal if absent. **(Q)** Lemmas 2, 3 (the row reads and writes only $n$'s presence and $\tau_n$).

**(C)** Row symbols: $\in$, $[\tau_n \mapsto \top]$. No arithmetic, no cardinality, no order. **Verified: cardinality-free.**

### C.3 O-Remove

Row: $\theta_n = \mathsf{Inactive}(-) \to$ undefined, $\Psi^t = \mathrm{id}_\Gamma$, edits $\mathrm{dom}(F_\gamma)$. Premises: $\tau_n = \top$; $\theta_n = \mathsf{Inactive}(-)$; $\forall m.\ \pi_m \neq n$.

**(W)** Window: $\tau_n$, the constructor of $\theta_n$, and for each present $m$ the equality $[\pi_m = n]$. Frame: the present-fiber index set of the childlessness fold.

**(K)** Per Appendix D.3's alphabet: one scalar occurrence $\langle \tau_n{=}\top?,\ \theta_n{=}\mathsf{Inactive}(-)? \rangle$ and one *child-witness occurrence* per $m$ with $\pi_m = n$ (recording only that a child exists; identity forgotten to the equality pattern).

**(G)** Premises 1–2 are scalar pattern matches, recorded atoms. Premise 3 is the negative existential fold of C.1's premise 4 with witness predicate $\pi_m = n$: it holds iff no child-witness occurrence is in the support. Function of the support; multiplicity (number of children) never read.

**(U)** $\widehat U = \{\mathsf{remove\mbox{-}entry}\}$: the one-point registry restriction $\gamma \smallsetminus n$. $\mathrm{lift}$ trivial; $\Psi = \mathrm{id}$. **(E)** none / refusal. **(Q)** Lemmas 2, 3; note for the frame that an added fiber with $\pi_m = n$ is a child witness and hence *inside* the window, as the hypothesis of Lemma 3 requires.

**(C)** Row symbols: $=\top$, pattern $\mathsf{Inactive}(-)$, $\forall$, $\neq$, $\smallsetminus$. No arithmetic, no cardinality. (The premise $\forall m.\ \pi_m \neq n$ counts nothing: it is an absence test.) **Verified: cardinality-free.**

### C.4 L-Begin

Row: $\theta_n = \mathsf{Inactive}(\bot) \to \mathsf{Reloading}(e_n, \mathrm{id}_\Gamma, \omega)$, $\Psi^t = \mathrm{id}_\Gamma$, edits $\theta_n$. Premises: $\theta_n = \mathsf{Inactive}(\bot)$; $\omega = \mathrm{target}_n(\gamma) \neq \bot$.

**(W)** Window: the constructor and outcome of $\theta_n$; $\tau_n$; $d_n$; and, for each $k \in d_n$, the provision status of $k$: whether some present fiber $m$ has $k \in \mathrm{dom}(\sigma_m)$ with $\theta_m = \mathsf{Active}(-,-)$ (the Active-provided case, with the provider's name as the committed edge's value), or $k \in p_m$ for some installed-but-not-Active $m$, or neither. Frame: the index set $d_n$ (a finite key set) for the target product.

**(K)** Per Appendix D.4's alphabet: one scalar occurrence $\langle \mathrm{cls}(\theta_n) \in \{\mathsf{Inactive}(\bot), \mathsf{Inactive}(\xi)\},\ \tau_n \rangle$; and one *per-key satisfaction occurrence* for each $k \in d_n$, of class $\mathsf{Active\mbox{-}provided}$, $\mathsf{installed\mbox{-}only\mbox{-}provided}$, or $\mathsf{unprovided}$, carrying the equality pattern of $k$ against the other read keys and — in the Active-provided class — the normalized committed-edge update (item (5) of Definition 6): $\mathsf{commit}(k \mapsto \text{provider-pattern})$.

**(G)** Premise 1 is the scalar pattern $\mathrm{cls}(\theta_n) = \mathsf{Inactive}(\bot)$ — note the outcome matters: $\mathsf{Inactive}(\xi)$ blocks ([SZC26] p. 38: "L-Begin has $\mathsf{Inactive}(\bot)$ as a premise, so the lifecycle is not re-entered from an error outcome"), which is why the class distinguishes $\bot$ from $\xi$. Premise 2 unfolds by Def. 46 into $\neg\tau_n$ (scalar atom) and $\gamma \vDash d_n$, i.e. every $k \in d_n$ lies in $\mathrm{dom}(\sigma_\gamma)$, i.e. — this is the *per-key satisfaction product*, written out once in full —

$$\gamma \vDash d_n \iff \bigwedge_{k \in d_n} \big[\text{the per-key occurrence at } k \text{ has class } \mathsf{Active\mbox{-}provided}\big].$$

*Left to right:* $k \in \mathrm{dom}(\sigma_\gamma)$ means (eq. 40) $k \in \mathrm{dom}(\sigma_m)$ for some Active $m$; that is the defining predicate of class $\mathsf{Active\mbox{-}provided}$. *Right to left:* immediate. The conjunction is over the occurrences of the multiset, one per key, and depends only on *which classes appear*: two keys of the same class contribute the same conjunct, so the guard is a function of the set of per-key classes present — "the set of classes present, not counts", now proved rather than drafted. The distinction $\mathsf{installed\mbox{-}only\mbox{-}provided}$ vs. $\mathsf{unprovided}$ is not read by this guard (both block); it is carried because Def. 49's note ([SZC26] p. 33: "a key that its transition has already written is therefore not yet one a dependent may activate against") makes the two classes reachable through observably different canonical scenarios, and the write-aspect bookkeeping of the successor report differs.

**(U)** $\widehat U_{\text{L-Begin}} = \{\theta \mapsto \mathsf{Reloading}(e_n, \mathrm{id}_\Gamma, \cdot)\} \cup \{\mathsf{commit}(k \mapsto \text{provider})\}$. The write is $[\theta_n \mapsto \mathsf{Reloading}(e_n, \mathrm{id}_\Gamma, \omega)]$ where $\omega = (k \in d_n) \mapsto \mathrm{provider}_k(\gamma)$ (Def. 46). $\omega$ is assembled by $\mathrm{lift}$ as the finite-map product $\bigsqcup_{k \in d_n} \{k \mapsto \mathrm{provider}_k(\gamma)\}$ over the frame's index set: each factor is the per-key occurrence's $\mathsf{commit}$ update; the factors have pairwise disjoint singleton supports, so the union is well defined and order-irrelevant (Definition 8(2)). $\mathrm{provider}_k(\gamma)$ is well defined *at each single key*, by Def. 58(2) (provisions of distinct present fibers are disjoint, so each provided key has exactly one Active provider — a singleton-witness fact, not a counting fact; Thm. 59 maintains the invariant). The scalar occurrence contributes the state write; $e_n$ and $\mathrm{id}_\Gamma$ are the fiber's own immutable field (Lemma 54(5)) and a reserved constant. $\Psi = \mathrm{id}$ per the row.

**(E)** No ordinary event at L-Begin itself (activation's $\mathsf{act}$ is emitted at L-Finish per the per-rule emission assignment; C.6(E)).

**(Q)** Equivariance: Lemma 2; the per-key reads are memberships and the committed edge stores a *name*, renamed coherently. Frame: Lemma 3 — an added fiber that provides some $k \in d_n$ changes the per-key class and hence lies inside the window; additions outside leave every per-key occurrence, and hence $\omega$, unchanged.

**(C)** Row symbols: pattern $\mathsf{Inactive}(\bot)$; $=$; $\neq \bot$; Def. 46's case split ($\tau_n \vee \neg(\gamma \vDash d_n)$, and the map $(k \in d_n) \mapsto \mathrm{provider}_k$); $[\theta_n \mapsto \cdots]$. $\vDash$ unfolds to memberships; $\mathrm{provider}$ to a singleton witness. No arithmetic, no cardinality, no order relation. **Verified: cardinality-free.**

### C.5 L-Iter

Row: $\theta_n = \mathsf{Reloading}(i,g,\omega) \to \mathsf{Reloading}(i', g \circ h, \omega)$, $\Psi^t = \mathrm{pr}_1 \circ i$, edits $\theta_n$. Premises: $\theta_n = \mathsf{Reloading}(i,g,\omega)$; $\mathrm{target}_n(\gamma) = \omega$; $i(\gamma) = (\delta, h, \mathsf{Just}(i'))$ (read with $\mathsf{Right}$ around the triple where §4.3.4 applies).

**(W)** Window: constructor of $\theta_n$ with its constituents $(i,g,\omega)$; $\tau_n$, $d_n$, and per-key provision statuses (through $\mathrm{target}_n$); the head constructor of the iterator $i$ — for the test-component language of Definition 2, the current step of the component script — with its payload; and the fields that step's operation reads (C.11). Frame: index set $d_n$ for the view comparison; the iterator position (order data); for a $\mathsf{register}$ step, the O-Insert frame of the drawn name.

**(K)** Per Appendix D.5's alphabet: one scalar occurrence $\langle \theta{=}\mathsf{Reloading}?,\ \text{view-relation},\ \text{next-step class} \rangle$ where the step classes are those of Appendix D's `StepClass` domain (with $\mathsf{raise}$-producing classes and $\mathsf{exhausted}$ selecting L-Raise resp. L-Finish rather than L-Iter — the three rules share this alphabet family and are separated by the class, which is how Appendix D partitions the drafts' pooled list); one *per-key view occurrence* for each $k \in d_n$, of class $\mathsf{agree}$ ($\mathrm{target}_n(\gamma)(k) = \omega(k)$), $\mathsf{differ}$, or — when $\mathrm{target}_n(\gamma) = \bot$ — the whole-view class $\bot$ recorded in the scalar occurrence; and the operation occurrences of C.11 for the landing step.

**(G)** Premise 1: scalar pattern. Premise 2 — the *per-key view-relation decomposition*, written out once in full, cited by C.6–C.8 and C.9 with only its polarity changed. $\mathrm{target}_n(\gamma)$ and $\omega$ are both maps $d_n \to \mathfrak N$ or $\bot$ (Defs. 44, 46). Case $\mathrm{target}_n(\gamma) = \bot$: recorded as the scalar view-relation class $\bot$; then premise 2 is false. Case $\mathrm{target}_n(\gamma) \neq \bot$: both maps are total on $d_n$ (the target by Def. 46's second branch; $\omega$ by Def. 58(3), preserved by Thm. 59), so

$$\mathrm{target}_n(\gamma) = \omega \iff \bigwedge_{k \in d_n} \big[\text{the view occurrence at } k \text{ has class } \mathsf{agree}\big],$$

a per-key conjunction of *name equalities* $\mathrm{provider}_k(\gamma) = \omega(k)$ — each key's conjunct recorded as that key's occurrence class. As with C.4's product, the conjunction depends only on the classes present. Premise 3: the head-constructor match on $i(\gamma)$, recorded as the next-step class; for the test-component language the step is data and the match is a constructor test; the payload conditions of the operation (preconditions of Defs. 23/29/31, the Alg.-6 outcome) are the C.11 occurrences.

**(U)** $\widehat U_{\text{L-Iter}} = \{\theta \mapsto \mathsf{Reloading}(\square, \square \circ h, \omega)\} \cup \widehat U_{\mathrm{op}}$ (C.11). The write has two parts per the row. (i) The state part $[\theta_n \mapsto \mathsf{Reloading}(i', g \circ h, \omega)]$: $i'$ is the continuation the landing yields; $g \circ h$ is the *binary composition operator* applied to the accumulator and the yielded inverse — this is the one order-sensitive operator of the row, and it is $\mathrm{lift}$'s clause (2) applied to frame order data: the new item is composed *onto* the accumulator ($g \circ h$, application order LIFO — [SZC26] p. 36, eq. 48 and the paragraph below it). CF3 constrains the implementation to use this same binary operator; the per-occurrence content is the single item $h$, and the induction to length-$m$ accumulators is the $m$-fold iteration of the binary operator by $\mathrm{lift}$, not a per-occurrence fact (Remark 2: CF3 extends the checked binary composition operator to accumulators of arbitrary finite length; the test alone does not make that induction). (ii) The context part $\Psi = \mathrm{pr}_1 \circ i$: one iterator landing, whose write is the landing operation's write — factored in C.11 into per-key/per-binding updates. For a $\mathsf{register}$ landing, the write is the O-Insert row at the drawn name (Def. 47), i.e. C.1's $\mathsf{new\mbox{-}entry}$ with $\pi = n$; the name is system-drawn (freshness premise satisfied by drawing, per Def. 47).

**(E)** By the per-rule emission assignment of the instrumented specification (Definition 1): $\mathsf{track}(t)$ lands with $\mathsf{app}(n,t)$; reads with $\mathsf{rd}(n,k,v)$ or $\mathsf{rderr}(n,k,\epsilon)$; $\mathsf{setval}$ with $\mathsf{val}(n,k)$; each a per-occurrence emission of the landing occurrence, concatenated by $\mathrm{lift}$ clause (3) (a single landing: one contribution).

**(Q)** Lemma 2 (the iterator payloads are carried sortwise; Definition 51's $\simeq$-witness condition transports the yielded triple across $\simeq$-related states — cited inside Lemma 55's conclusion paragraph); Lemma 3.

**(C)** Row symbols: pattern $\mathsf{Reloading}(i,g,\omega)$; $=$ (view equality — per-key name equalities); constructor match $\mathsf{Just}$/$\mathsf{Nothing}$/$\mathsf{Left}$; $\circ$ (composition); $\mathrm{pr}_1$. The operation equations add memberships and map updates (C.11(C)). No symbol of the row is arithmetic; no cardinality of any collection is read — in particular the rule does not read $\mathrm{len}(i)$: the remaining-iteration count appears only in the *metatheory's* bound (Thm. 66's $\mathrm{len}(e_n) \le K$), never in a premise. **Verified: cardinality-free.**

### C.7 L-Divert

Row: $\theta_n = \mathsf{Reloading}(i,g,\omega) \to \mathsf{Unloading}(g \circ h, \omega, \bot)$, $\Psi^t = \mathrm{id}_\Gamma$ or $\mathrm{pr}_1 \circ i$, edits $\theta_n$. Premises: $\theta_n = \mathsf{Reloading}(i,g,\omega)$; $\mathrm{target}_n(\gamma) \neq \omega$; $(\delta,h) = (\gamma, \mathrm{id}_\Gamma) \vee i(\gamma) = (\delta,h,-)$.

**(W)/(K)** As C.5, with: the view-relation class read at polarity $\neq$ — the decomposition of C.5 negated, i.e. the premise holds iff the scalar view class is $\bot$ or some per-key occurrence has class $\mathsf{differ}$ (an *existential* over per-key disagreement witnesses; function of the support, multiplicity of differing keys never read — the two directions of change are not distinguished, [SZC26] p. 46: "a target view that has become $\bot$ and one that has become some other fiber are equally unequal to $\omega$"); and one additional scalar atom, the *alternative* $\mathsf{alt} \in \{\mathsf{abort}, \mathsf{land}\}$, recording which disjunct of premise 3 the step takes. The two alternatives are two entries of the decision — both offered by $\mathbb S$ at the base layer, with the $\mathsf{land}$-only restriction under the inertia of §4.3.3 ([SZC26] p. 37: the layer "takes the form of a restriction on which alternative of L-Divert a host may take"); Appendix D.7 records per occurrence which alternatives are reachable at the instrumented interface **(D-D again)**, and the certificate names the alternative taken.

**(G)** Scalar pattern + negated view decomposition (above) + for $\mathsf{alt} = \mathsf{land}$ the constructor condition that $i(\gamma)$ yields a triple.

**(U)** $\widehat U = \{\theta \mapsto \mathsf{Unloading}(\square, \omega, \bot)\} \cup \{\theta \mapsto \mathsf{Unloading}(\square \circ h, \omega, \bot)\} \cup \widehat U_{\mathrm{op}}$: at $\mathsf{abort}$, $h = \mathrm{id}_\Gamma$, $\Psi = \mathrm{id}_\Gamma$, the accumulator passes unchanged ($g \circ \mathrm{id} = g$; Table 1's fourth column "$\mathrm{id}_\Gamma$ or $\mathrm{pr}_1 \circ i$" and the note that $h$ is $\mathrm{id}_\Gamma$ where L-Divert aborts); at $\mathsf{land}$, exactly C.5's two-part write with $\mathsf{Unloading}(\cdot,\cdot,\bot)$ as the state constructor. **(E)** the landing's events at $\mathsf{land}$; none at $\mathsf{abort}$. **(Q)** as C.5. **(C)** Row symbols: C.5's plus $\vee$ and $\mathrm{id}_\Gamma$; no arithmetic; **verified: cardinality-free.**

### C.8 L-Raise

Row: $\theta_n = \mathsf{Reloading}(i,g,\omega) \to \mathsf{Unloading}(g,\omega,\xi)$, $\Psi^t = \mathrm{id}_\Gamma$, edits $\theta_n$. Premises: $\theta_n = \mathsf{Reloading}(i,g,\omega)$; $i(\gamma) = \mathsf{Left}(\xi)$.

**(W)/(K)** Window: $\theta_n$'s constructor and constituents; the head of $i$ (the raising step). Per Appendix D.8's alphabet: one scalar occurrence $\langle \theta{=}\mathsf{Reloading}?, \text{step class} \in \text{Left-producing classes}, \text{error pattern of } \xi \rangle$ plus, for write-aspect bookkeeping of the *subsequent* L-Unload's observable $\mathsf{inv}$ word, one occurrence per inverse class present in $g$ (classes as in C.10). Note the rule is *not conditioned on the target view at all* (its premises name no target; Appendix D.8's ambiguity note A2 records the resulting L-Divert/L-Raise overlap), so no view occurrences enter the guard.

**(G)** Two scalar atoms (state pattern; $\mathsf{Left}$ constructor match). **(U)** $\widehat U = \{\theta \mapsto \mathsf{Unloading}(g,\omega,\xi)\}$: the accumulator passes *unchanged* ($\Psi = \mathrm{id}_\Gamma$; the accumulator built up to the failing iteration is applied later, at L-Unload — [SZC26] p. 38, "L-Raise recovers before it records"). The error $\xi$ is carried as an opaque payload into the outcome. **(E)** none at the raise itself (the failing iteration's raise is the absence of a landing event; error surfacing is the report's failure class and outcome). **(Q)** Lemmas 2/3; $\xi$ is compared by no premise. **(C)** Row symbols: state pattern, $\mathsf{Left}$ match, field write. No arithmetic; **verified: cardinality-free.**

### C.9 L-Leave

**(W)/(K)** Per Appendix D.9's alphabet: one scalar occurrence $\langle \theta{=}\mathsf{Active}?, \text{view-relation} \rangle$ and the per-key view occurrences of C.5, read at polarity $\neq$ exactly as in C.7 (the decomposition and its support-only dependence are cited, not repeated). **(U)** $\widehat U = \{\theta \mapsto \mathsf{Unloading}(g,\omega,\bot)\}$: one constructor rewrite carrying $g, \omega$ unchanged; $\Psi = \mathrm{id}$. No arithmetic; **verified: cardinality-free.**

### C.10 L-Unload

Row: $\theta_n = \mathsf{Unloading}(g,\omega,\zeta) \to \mathsf{Inactive}(\zeta)$, $\Psi^t = g$, edits $\theta_n$. Premises: $\theta_n = \mathsf{Unloading}(g,\omega,\zeta)$; $\neg\mathrm{relied}_n(\gamma)$; $g(\gamma) = \delta$.

**(W)** Window: $\theta_n$'s constructor and constituents (the accumulator $g$ *as a finite list of composed inverses with its composition order* — frame order data; the committed view $\omega$; the outcome $\zeta$); $\tau_n$ (bookkeeping, below); for the guard, for every present installed $m \neq n$ and $k \in d_m$, the equality $[\omega_m(k) = n]$; and the fields each inverse writes.

**(K)** Per Appendix D.10's alphabet: one scalar occurrence $\langle \theta{=}\mathsf{Unloading}?,\ \mathrm{cls}(\zeta) \in \{\bot,\xi\},\ \tau_n,\ \text{post-target class} \in \{\bot, \mathsf{satisfiable}\} \rangle$; one *reliance-witness occurrence* per pair $(m,k)$ with $m \neq n$, $\mathrm{installed}_m(\gamma)$, $k \in d_m$, $\omega_m(k) = n$ (the existential-witness fold of C.1, witness predicate = Def. 50's matrix); and one *per-item occurrence* for each item of the accumulator list, of class $\mathsf{provide}^{-1}$, $\mathsf{track}^{-1}(t\text{-pattern})$, $\mathsf{register}^{-1}$, or $\mathsf{read\mbox{-}on\mbox{-}revert}$. Of the scalar atoms, only the state pattern and (through the witnesses) the guard are premise reads; $\mathrm{cls}(\zeta)$ is a *write input* (it lands in $\mathsf{Inactive}(\zeta)$); $\tau_n$ and the post-target class are *verdict bookkeeping* — read by the canonical experiment's successor settle and report, not by this rule — carried in $\Sigma_r$ so that the retirement and provider-loss unloads are distinct signatures (they must be: Proposition 1 turns on exactly this distinction).

### C.11 Operation clauses inside iterator landings

The landing classes $\mathsf{provide}$, $\mathsf{read}$, $\mathsf{setval}$, and the orchestration inputs $\mathsf{isolate}/\mathsf{intercept}$ route through the coeffect operations, whose defining equations are Defs. 23/24 (get/set), 28/29 (isolation), 30/31 (interception), and Algorithm 6 (proxy-mediated access). We verify that each defining equation factors through the operation occurrences and is cardinality-free; Appendix D.11–D.14 **(D-A)** fix the exact per-operation alphabets.

1. **$\mathsf{set}$/$\mathsf{provide}$ (Def. 23, eq. 21; Def. 29 eq. 28; Def. 31 eq. 30).** $\mathsf{set}(k,v)$ requires $k \notin \mathrm{dom}(\sigma)$ (fresh) and writes $\sigma[k \mapsto v]$ with inverse $\lambda\sigma'.\ \sigma' \smallsetminus k$; under isolation the precondition and write are transported along $\rho$ ($\rho(k) \notin \mathrm{dom}(\sigma)$, write at $\rho(k)$). The guard atom is the single membership — the classes $\mathsf{provide(fresh)}$ vs. $\mathsf{provide(conflict)}$ — a per-binding fact; the write and inverse touch the one binding ("Typing an operation of $k$ on $\mathcal V_k$ is what confines it to the binding at $k$: the lift reads and writes that binding and leaves every other key as it stands, so no side condition is needed to say so" — Def. 24's commentary). Function of the singleton occurrence; equation contains $\notin$, $[\cdot \mapsto \cdot]$, $\smallsetminus$ — no arithmetic.
2. **$\mathsf{get}$/operation application (Def. 23/24, eqs. 21–23).** $\mathsf{get}(k)$ requires $k \in \mathrm{dom}(\sigma)$ and returns $\sigma(k)$; an operation $a \in \mathcal A_k$ acts on the value alone and lifts by eq. 23 to read and write the binding at $k$ and nothing else. Occurrence: the binding's presence and the *own/foreign* atom (whether $k \in d_n$ resp. $k \in p_n$ — the confinement classes Def. 48 reads). Per-binding; no arithmetic (the equations contain $\mathbf{let}$, application, $[\cdot\mapsto\cdot]$).
3. **$\mathsf{read}$ through Algorithm 6.** The resolution walks the fiber chain upward from the accessing context and takes the *first* of three exits: (a) $k \in \mathrm{fiber.committed}$ — return the committed binding ($\mathsf{rd}(n,k,v)$; classes $\mathsf{read(committed)}$ at the own fiber and $\mathsf{read(ancestor\mbox{-}committed)}$ at a proper ancestor — the *ancestry-position class*, an order-pattern over the finitely many chain positions the equality pattern distinguishes, not a depth count); (b) $k \in \mathrm{fiber.inject}$ without commitment — $\mathsf{rderr}(n,k,\mathsf{IA})$, class $\mathsf{read(declared\mbox{-}uncommitted)}$; (c) root reached — $\mathsf{rderr}(n,k,\mathsf{UD})$, class $\mathsf{read(undeclared)}$. Each loop iteration reads two memberships and one equality-with-root; the *outcome* is determined by the classes present along the chain and their relative order pattern, which the occurrence records as its ancestry-position class. The walk reads no count (its length is bounded by the parent tree, but no premise mentions that bound). Reading the view rather than the store is what makes this a window read of $\omega$-fields — consistent with C.5's window. No arithmetic.
4. **$\mathsf{isolate}$ (Def. 29).** $\mathsf{isolate}(k,r)$ writes $\rho[k \mapsto r]$, deriving a context; no precondition ("a key already isolated is reassigned rather than refused"). Occurrence: the realm-relation atom — same/joined/distinct — the equality pattern among $\rho(k)$, $r$, $k$. Single-binding write; no arithmetic.
5. **$\mathsf{intercept}$ (Def. 31).** $\mathsf{intercept}(k,\nu)$ writes $\iota[k \mapsto \iota(k) \oplus_k \nu]$; $\mathsf{get}(k,\mu)$ evaluates $\sigma(k)(\mu \oplus_k \iota(k))$. The monoid fold $\oplus_k$ is associative with unit $\epsilon_k$; the occurrence records the *order pattern* of the (at most two, per Appendix D.13's cap) merged metadata sources — component-declared and context-carried, right-biased — not their number: the depth-2 order-pattern cap is justified in Appendix D.13 by associativity (every deeper chain is a $\oplus$ of a depth-2 pattern), and the factorization itself is insensitive to the choice: $\oplus_k$ is a binary operator iterated by lift exactly as the accumulator composition of C.5, and the per-occurrence content is one merge. No arithmetic in eqs. 29–30.

**Conclusion of C.11.** Every landing's guard is a Boolean function of the operation occurrence(s); every write is a single-binding update or a lift-iterated binary fold; every equation-symbol inventory is free of arithmetic and cardinality reads. $\square$
