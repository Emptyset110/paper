### 6.4 Safety

**Lemma 6 (table equality).** Let $\mathbb I \in \mathfrak I_{\mathrm{CF}}$ and $\mathbb I \models T^\star$. Then $f_r^{\mathbb I}(s) = f_r^{\mathbb S}(s)$ for every $r \in \mathrm{Rules}$ and every $s \in \Sigma_r^{\mathrm{reach}}$.

*Proof.* Fix $(r,s)$ with $s$ reachable. By **(D-C)**, $T^\star$ contains the canonical experiment $(\rho_{r,s}, V_{r,s})$ — this is the surjectivity of the canonical-realization table onto reachable signatures, which Appendix D executes. $\mathbb I \models T^\star$ gives $\mathbb I \models (\rho_{r,s}, V_{r,s})$; the contrapositive of Lemma 5 gives $f_r^{\mathbb I}(s) = f_r^{\mathbb S}(s)$. $\square$

**Definition 9 (tagged executions and matched prefixes).** A *tagged execution* of $\mathbb I$ on a script $\rho$ is a finite sequence $e = u_0 \xrightarrow{\ell_1} u_1 \xrightarrow{\ell_2} \cdots \xrightarrow{\ell_m} u_m$ of implementation transitions from the initial state, where each $\ell_i$ is, per CF1's complete tagging, either an orchestration input of $\rho$ (accepted or refused), a lifecycle tag $(r_i, n_i)$, or an *untagged* internal step (which by CF1 changes no abstract field and emits nothing). A specification execution $d = \gamma_0 \to \cdots \to \gamma_{m'}$ *matches* $e$ *up to $j$ under $(\chi, \cdot)$* when there is a sort-preserving bijection $\chi$ and a monotone surjection from the tagged steps among $\ell_1..\ell_j$ onto $d$'s steps such that corresponding steps carry $\chi$-corresponding labels (same rule, $\chi$-related fiber; same input verdict), $\equiv$-equal emissions and certificates, and $\gamma_i \simeq \chi^{-1}\cdot A(u_{\iota(i)})$ at every corresponding pair, with untagged steps of $e$ matched to nothing.

**Definition 10 (deviation; the well-order).** A *deviation of type F (forward)* is a triple $(\rho, e, j)$ where $e$ is a tagged execution of $\mathbb I$ on $\rho$, its first $j-1$ steps are matched by some specification execution, but no specification execution matches its first $j$ steps — including the sub-cases: step $j$'s tag/decision/update/emission/certificate/refusal disagrees with every $\mathbb S$-continuation; step $j$ is an untagged step that changes an abstract field or emits (excluded outright by CF1, listed for exhaustiveness); or $u_j$ is quiescent-reported with a report no matched $\mathbb S$-quiescent state's report $\equiv$-equals. A *deviation of type B (backward)* is a triple $(\rho, e, j)$ where $e$'s first $j$ steps are matched by a specification execution $d$, and some transition of $\mathbb S$ enabled at $d$'s endpoint (an accepted input of $\rho$'s next action, or an enabled lifecycle rule during a settle) has no correspondingly labeled transition or refusal offered by $\mathbb I$ at $u_j$. Well-order: deviations are ordered by their index $j \in \mathbb N$; $(\mathbb N, \le)$ is a well-order, so if any deviation (of either type) exists, one with minimal $j$ exists. (The script $\rho$ and execution $e$ at the minimum are not unique and need not be; minimality of $j$ is all the argument uses. We do not order by execution length: the steps after $j$ are irrelevant, and truncating at $j$ keeps $j$ fixed.)

**Theorem 4 (safety).** Let $\mathbb I \in \mathfrak I_{\mathrm{CF}}$ and $\mathbb I \models T^\star$. Then no deviation of either type exists. Consequently, for every script $\rho$: every finite tagged execution of $\mathbb I$ on $\rho$ is matched in full by a specification execution (every finite behavior of $\mathbb I$ is one of $\mathbb S$), and every finite specification execution is matched by an offered execution of $\mathbb I$.

*Proof.* Suppose a deviation exists and fix one, $(\rho, e, j)$, with $j$ minimal, of type F or B. By minimality, the first $j-1$ steps of $e$ (type F) resp. $j$ steps (type B) are matched; fix a matching specification execution $d$ with endpoint $\gamma$ and renaming $\chi$, so that

$$\gamma \;\simeq\; \chi^{-1}\cdot A(u_{j-1}) \quad (\text{type F, writing } u_{j-1} \text{ for the state step } j \text{ leaves from; type B: } \gamma \simeq \chi^{-1}\cdot A(u_j)).$$

**The reachability bridge — spelled out.** The deviating configuration is reached by $\mathbb I$, and $\kappa_r$, $\Sigma_r^{\mathrm{reach}}$, and $T^\star$ are all defined over *specification* windows. Three facts bridge the gap, and each is genuinely needed. (i) CF1's abstraction is total on $\mathrm{Reach}(\mathbb I)$, so $A(u_{j-1})$ *exists* as an abstract registry — without CF1 the deviating state would have no signature to speak of. (ii) $A(u_{j-1})$ by itself need **not** be $\mathbb S$-reachable: CF1 constrains $A$'s totality and field preservation, not its image. It is the induction hypothesis — the matched prefix — that supplies $\mathbb S$-reachability: $\gamma$ is the endpoint of a genuine specification execution from the empty registry, hence reachable, and $\gamma \simeq \chi^{-1} \cdot A(u_{j-1})$. (iii) Lemma 1 transports signatures across exactly this gap: for any rule $r$ and fiber $n$,

$$\kappa_r\big(W_r(A(u_{j-1}), n)\big) = \kappa_r\big(W_r(\gamma, \chi^{-1}n)\big),$$

and the right-hand window sits at a reachable specification state, so **(D-B)** every occurrence in it lies in $\Sigma_r^{\mathrm{reach}}$. This is the only point in the whole development where membership of the *implementation's* current occurrences in the *reachable* alphabet is established, and it holds only because steps $< j$ agreed; a deviation at an earlier step could have marooned $A(u)$ at an abstract state outside $\mathrm{Reach}(\mathbb S)$ whose occurrences $T^\star$ never pinned. Minimality is thus not bookkeeping — it is the hypothesis that keeps the table applicable.

**Type F, step $j$ tagged $(r,n)$ (lifecycle) or an input of constructor $r$ (orchestration).** By the bridge, every occurrence $s \in \kappa_r(W_r(A(u_{j-1}), n))$ is reachable; by Lemma 6, $f_r^{\mathbb I}(s) = f_r^{\mathbb S}(s)$ at each. Apply Lemma 4 in the instance of Remark 2: $W = W_r(A(u_{j-1}), n)$, $W' = W_r(\gamma, \chi^{-1}n)$, the frames corresponding under $(\chi, \mathrm{id})$ because the windows are $\simeq$-related images of one another (Lemma 1 gives both the multiset equality and the frame correspondence). Clause (3) plus table equality yields: $\mathbb I$'s decision, refusal reason, assembled abstract update, emitted word, and certificate at step $j$ are carried by $\chi$ to exactly $\mathbb S$'s at $(\gamma, \chi^{-1}n)$ — where "$\mathbb S$'s" means the result of $f_r^{\mathbb S}$-with-$\mathrm{lift}_r$, which by Theorem 3 (CF3 for $\mathbb S$) *is* the specification's transition relation at that state. Moreover the successor abstractions re-relate: $A(u_j)$'s abstract update equals the specification successor's up to $\simeq/\approx$ (Lemma 4(3)), so $d$ extended by the corresponding $\mathbb S$-step matches $e$'s first $j$ steps. That contradicts "$(\rho,e,j)$ is a type-F deviation". The sub-cases fall with it: a tag/decision disagreement is excluded by the decision equality; an emission/certificate disagreement by the word equality; a refusal disagreement by the reason equality.

**Type F, step $j$ untagged.** By CF1 an untagged step changes no abstract field and emits nothing, so it cannot make the prefix unmatched (Definition 9 matches untagged steps to nothing and compares only abstract data); this sub-case is empty.

**Type F, report mismatch at quiescence.** $u_j = u_{j-1}$ quiescent-reported. Reports are functions of the abstract state's report fields (CF1's preservation clause) and $\gamma \simeq \chi^{-1}\cdot A(u_{j-1})$; $\simeq$-related states have $\equiv$-equal reports (eq. 53 keeps every report-visible field up to the value equivalences $\equiv$ absorbs; the refused-input log agrees by the matched prefix's refusal agreement). And $\gamma$ is *specification*-quiescent iff $A(u_{j-1})$ is ($\mathrm{quiet}$ is a function of the fields $\simeq$ preserves, Lemma 55's premise inventory applied to eq. 45); $\mathbb I$'s settle returns exactly at abstract quiescence by CF6. So the reports match; sub-case empty.

**Type B.** Let the unmatched enabled $\mathbb S$-transition at $\gamma$ be an application of $r$ at $\chi^{-1}n$ (or an accepted input). By Theorem 3 (CF3 for $\mathbb S$), its enablement means $\mathrm{lift}_r$'s guard passes on $\{f_r^{\mathbb S}(s)\}$ over $\kappa_r(W_r(\gamma, \chi^{-1}n))$. By the bridge and Lemma 6 the same guard passes on $\{f_r^{\mathbb I}(s)\}$ over the $\kappa$-equal window at $A(u_j)$, so by CF3 $\mathbb I$'s $r$-transition at $(u_j, n)$ is enabled — i.e. *offered*: CF3's enablement is the implementation's transition relation, and Definition 3's clause 3 makes offering, not scheduling, the content of the clause; fairness (CF6) separately guarantees the offer is realized in some explored run, which is what $\mathrm{Out}$-set claims need (§6.6). With the offer present and (by the type-F argument) agreeing in decision, update, and emission, the $\mathbb S$-transition is matched. Contradiction.

Both types are refuted, so no deviation exists. The two consequences restate the definitions: absence of type-F deviations at every $j$ gives, by induction on $j$ (the matching of Definition 9 extends step by step), a full match for every finite tagged execution; absence of type-B deviations gives the converse offering. $\square$
